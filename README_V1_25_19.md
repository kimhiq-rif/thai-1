# Thai Trainer v1.25.19

## Load Challenge Bonus

- Replaced the short 10-question daily bonus with a real load challenge.
- The challenge unlocks after the regular daily practice goal is complete.
- The student must press `Start challenge`; the two-hour timer starts only then.
- Rules:
  - 2 hours
  - 50 answered questions
  - accuracy must be above 70%
  - at least 10 answers must be Level 1.2
  - reward: 15 coach points toward the next skin
- If 50 questions are answered but accuracy is too low and time remains, the student can continue answering to improve accuracy.
- Time warnings appear at 15 minutes and 5 minutes remaining.
- The challenge resets with the next daily practice day.
