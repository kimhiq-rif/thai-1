v1.21 — Local eraser tool
- Added a toggle button next to Clear / Show Answer.
- The button shows a pencil-eraser SVG icon.
- First click turns eraser mode on; second click returns to pen mode.
- Active eraser mode is darker and glowing.
- Eraser removes only the touched stroke area on the writing canvas, not the whole canvas.
- Works with pointer events, touch events for older iPad Safari, and mouse fallback.
