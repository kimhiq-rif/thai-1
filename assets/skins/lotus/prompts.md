# Lotus Sentinel character

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the Thai Trainer
`Lotus Temple` M0 pilot. The project assets were produced from a flat green
chroma-key source, locally converted to alpha, and exported as transparent
WebP at 1024x1536 and 512x768.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Full-body premium mobile-game character cutout:
an original adult Thai Lotus Sentinel scholar-warrior in layered lotus-petal
ceremonial fantasy armor. Calm, confident mentor pose; one open hand presents
a faceted lotus crystal and the other welcomes the learner. Polished 3D game
illustration; amethyst, magenta, blush, pale antique gold and moon ivory;
crisp silhouette and generous portrait padding. No copyrighted character,
deity, monk, living person, weapon, wings, religious symbol, text, logo or
watermark. Generated on a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background with
no cast shadow or floor plane.
